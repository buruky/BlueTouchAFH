# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Families in the Everett, WA area (Silver Lake neighborhood and surrounding) searching for adult family home care for an aging or dependent relative. They are evaluating trust, warmth, and day-to-day quality of life, usually comparing a small number of licensed homes before scheduling a tour.

## Product Purpose

Blue Touch is a family-owned, licensed Washington State Adult Family Home operating two homes in Everett's Silver Lake neighborhood. The site exists to earn a phone call or scheduled tour from a family evaluating care options for a loved one.

## Positioning

A small, personally-managed home (owned and run day-to-day by Titi Tekle) rather than an institutional facility chain: a known caregiver, a known routine, home-cooked meals, and a resident count small enough that staff know each person by name.

## Operating Context

Two physical homes: 108th Street Home (309 108th St SW, Everett) and 27th Drive Home (12907 27th Dr SE, Everett), each with its own real interior/exterior photography in `src/assets/location-a` and `src/assets/location-b`. Visitors browse on both mobile and desktop; conversion actions are "call" and "schedule a tour."

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No booking system; contact is by phone/email only.
- No verified pricing, resident capacity numbers, or named caregiving staff beyond the owner — do not invent these.
- No testimonials, reviews, or case studies on hand — do not fabricate them.
- Real photography exists only for home interiors/exteriors; no photos of the owner, caregivers, or residents (privacy).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Blue Touch." Palette and type system ("Charcoal & Azure": navy + azure/gold accent, Spectral display serif + Source Sans 3 body) were deliberately locked in and must be preserved.
- Real home photography is the only imagery authority; it must be kept and used, not replaced.
- Tone: warm, trustworthy, family-run — never clinical/institutional, never startup-flashy.

## Evidence on Hand

- `src/data/content.js`: business contact info, two locations with address/phone/blurb/highlights, amenities list, values list — all real, confirmed facts.
- Real interior/exterior photography per location (9–10 images each) under `src/assets/location-a` and `src/assets/location-b`.
- Washington State Adult Family Homes are licensed/regulated by WA DSHS — public regulatory fact, usable for a licensing mention without inventing a license number.

## Product Principles

1. Trust and warmth outrank cleverness — every decision should read as "a family that would take care of my parent," not "a startup."
2. Never fabricate people, quotes, stats, or pricing; only real content or honest, general process information.
3. The real home photography is the hero of the page — layout and motion should showcase it, not compete with it.
4. Keep the locked Charcoal & Azure identity (palette, type) as the authority; invention happens in layout, structure, motion, and authored decorative assets.
